Police discovered evidence of multiple violent deaths, deliberate scene staging, anda concealed network connecting a private rehabilitation clinic, a transport company, a nightclub, several shell businesses, and a small extortion ring. An extortion and blackmail operation targeting clients with access to money or sensitive information. 3. A smaller inner group that selected victims, controlled access to the Ashwood property, and staged evidence to redirect suspicion toward expendable associates.

Investigators must distinguish confirmed facts from Al-generated hypotheses and unresolved contradictions.
